About

I'm a neuroscientist and computer vision engineer working at the interface of AI and the brain to implement therapeutic technology that enhances cognitive well-being and extends human agency.

My research asks a basic question: how does a system — biological or artificial — make sense of the world and interact in it? I use computational models to design experiments around this question, and validate their predictions against human behavior and high-resolution brain signals. I care most about building research programs that accelerate scientific investigations and their translation into impactful applications that can improve our quality of life.

Approach
Model-guided experimental design — model-to-brain and model-to-behavior comparison, mechanistic interpretability.
Data generation
Chronic laminar electrode arrays sustained beyond a year in deep-sulcal recording; large-scale non-human-primate in vivo electrophysiology + automating software preprocessing pipelines. VR kinematics tracking visual inputs and hand gestures for object manipulations
Engineering
Deep-learning & computer-vision decoding; Mechanistic interpretability. Dynamical / recurrent models whose internal dynamics reproduce real population recordings.
